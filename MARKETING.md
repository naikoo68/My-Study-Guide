# Marketing & Growth Checklist (SEO Step 10)

The technical SEO setup (steps 1–9) is done: clean URLs, robots.txt, sitemap,
per-page titles, Google Analytics, Search Console, professional email, and
subject/stream landing pages. **Step 10 is ongoing marketing** — this is your
playbook. Tick items as you go.

Site: **https://www.mystudyguide.in**

---

## A. Use the tools already built into the app
- [ ] **Rich share links** — the app generates link previews at `/s/:token`. Share individual quizzes/tests into WhatsApp/Telegram groups; they show a rich card (subject, name, first question) that pulls people in.
- [ ] **Referral codes** — you have a referral reward system. Promote *"invite a friend — you both get free days."*
- [ ] **Facebook Auto-Post** — turn it ON in the admin panel so new quizzes/notices auto-post to your Facebook Page daily (fresh, consistent content = more reach + SEO signals).
- [ ] **Public share links for streams/subjects** — share your new `/subjects/...` pages directly.

## B. Social presence (set up once, then post consistently)
- [ ] Create/complete pages: **Facebook, Instagram, Telegram channel, WhatsApp channel, YouTube**.
- [ ] Put `www.mystudyguide.in` in **every** bio/about section.
- [ ] Add all social URLs in **Admin → Customization** so they show in your site footer (internal links + outbound signals).
- [ ] Posting ideas: "Question of the day", weekly mock-test announcements, exam news & dates (e.g. JKSSB / JK exams), quick tips, result screenshots from students.

## C. Free backlinks (one-time — these help ranking)
- [ ] **Google Business Profile** — free listing; helps branded/local search.
- [ ] Free directories: **Crunchbase, Product Hunt, Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART** (free tiers), **Google Sites**.
- [ ] **Quora / Reddit** (r/india, exam-specific subreddits) — genuinely answer questions and link the relevant `/subjects/...` page where it actually helps.
- [ ] Get listed in **student WhatsApp/Telegram group directories**.
- [ ] Ask any coaching institutes/teachers you know to link to the site.

## D. Content that ranks (ongoing)
- [ ] Publish exam-specific content: *"JKSSB syllabus 2026"*, *"Best mock tests for <exam>"* — and link to your `/subjects/...`, `/quiz`, and `/test-series` pages.
- [ ] Keep every **subject description** filled in — those are now your on-page SEO copy (they show on the `/subjects/:slug` pages).
- [ ] Give each new stream/subject a clear name + description so its landing page ranks for that term.

## E. Measure & iterate (check weekly)
- [ ] **Google Analytics** → Reports → **Acquisition**: which channels bring visitors.
- [ ] **Google Search Console** → **Performance**: which pages/queries get impressions & clicks; **Pages/Coverage**: what's indexed.
- [ ] Re-submit the sitemap and **Request indexing** for new important pages (e.g. `/subjects`, top subjects).

---

## Quick wins to do first
1. Turn on **Facebook Auto-Post** in the admin panel.
2. Add your **social links** in Admin → Customization.
3. Create a **Google Business Profile**.
4. Share 3–5 **quiz/subject links** into relevant WhatsApp/Telegram groups this week.
5. Post a "Question of the day" daily for 2 weeks and watch Analytics.
